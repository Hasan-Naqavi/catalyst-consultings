# Catalyst Consulting
**Healthcare spend, made measurable.**

Catalyst Consulting is a financial consultancy for healthcare and life-sciences companies.
We help clients prove which commercial spend creates value, so they can invest with
confidence in the treatments patients need.

**Our values:** Diversity · Sincerity · Innovation

## What we do
- **Investment appraisal** – NPV, IRR and break-even analysis
- **Channel ROI** – which sales, education, digital and patient-support spend drives uptake
- **NHS market access** – models that reflect clinical guidelines and real NHS uptake

## Client project: MSD
**Brief:** Does £120m a year of commercial investment in a new chronic-condition therapy
create value, and how should it be split across channels?
